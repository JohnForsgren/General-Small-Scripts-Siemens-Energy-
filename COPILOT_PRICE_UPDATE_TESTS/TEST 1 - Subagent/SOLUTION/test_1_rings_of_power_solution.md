# Rings of Power Season 2

## Episode Summaries
- Episode 1: Sauron returns in a new form, the three Elven Rings restore the Elves, and he heads toward Eregion while Adar prepares for war.
- Episode 2: As disasters spread across Middle-earth, Sauron deceives Celebrimbor in Eregion by presenting himself as Annatar, the "Lord of Gifts".
- Episode 3: Isildur survives in Mordor, Pharazon gains support in Numenor, and Celebrimbor and Annatar secure mithril from Durin III for more rings.
- Episode 4: Galadriel is captured by Adar after Elrond escapes with Nenya, while the Stranger meets Tom Bombadil and other journeys continue across Middle-earth.
- Episode 5: Durin III is corrupted by his ring, Annatar pressures Celebrimbor into helping forge rings for Men, and Elrond warns Gil-galad of danger.
- Episode 6: Celebrimbor concentrates on the rings for Men while Annatar manages Eregion, and Adar begins the siege after Galadriel realizes Sauron's plan.
- Episode 7: During the assault on Eregion, Celebrimbor discovers Annatar's deception, gives the nine rings to Galadriel, and Adar captures Nenya after stabbing Arondir.
- Episode 8: Sauron kills Celebrimbor and seizes the nine rings as the survivors of Eregion escape and resolve to continue resisting him.

## Celebrimbor Notes
- Celebrimbor does not know Halbrand is Sauron when he approaches him in Eregion.
- Halbrand convinces Celebrimbor that he is Annatar, the "Lord of Gifts", and an emissary from the Valar.
- Celebrimbor and Annatar ask the Dwarves for mithril so they can make more Rings of Power.
- Celebrimbor refuses at first to be involved in forging Rings of Power for Men.
- Celebrimbor agrees to help make rings for Men in an attempt to redeem his work on the Dwarven rings.
- Annatar gives Celebrimbor a container that he claims holds mithril.
- Celebrimbor later realizes he has made the nine rings using Sauron's blood rather than mithril.
- Celebrimbor attempts to escape with the nine rings and gives Galadriel the chance to take them.
- Sauron kills Celebrimbor in Eregion.
